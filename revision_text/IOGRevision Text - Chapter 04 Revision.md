## Chapter 04 Translation Analysis

### Line 2-8: Travel menu
**Japanese**: どこへ むかいますか？ >やめる >エドワード城
- どこへ = "where"
- むかいますか = "are you heading/going?"
- やめる = "quit/cancel"
- エドワード城 = "Edward Castle"

**Jerauld**: "Where do you go? >Quit >Edward's Castle" - **Silver**  
- Right: Accurate meaning
- Wrong: "do you go" awkward tense, possessive "'s" not needed

**Swimmy**: "Where are you going? >Quit >Edward Castle" - **Gold**  
- Right: Natural present progressive, clean castle name

**Thammy**: "Where are you heading? / Quit / Edward Castle" - **Gold**  
- Right: "heading" good alternative, clean format

**Recommendation**: Use **Swimmy** verbatim
